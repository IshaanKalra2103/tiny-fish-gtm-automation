About us

I'm Daniel Ortiz. I opened Bean & Brick in 2019 after ten years as a barista, and I still pull shots most mornings. We roast with a local roaster and bake our pastries each day. We change our specials often and post them on Instagram (@beanandbrick), so check there before you come. Free wifi, but no laptops on Saturdays after 10.
