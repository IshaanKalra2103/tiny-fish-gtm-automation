# PRD: AI-Native GTM Automation Machine

## 1. Goal

Build a self-healing, end-to-end GTM system that discovers social trends, learns winning marketing patterns, generates videos, validates them with AI personas, publishes approved content, and improves using engagement data.

## 2. One-hour demo

Demonstrate one complete loop: analyze a trend using TinyFish, visualize findings, extract a reusable content template, generate a short video, run persona-based QA, revise a failed draft, and show the approved post ready to publish.

## 3. Final product

A Paperclip-inspired agentic company harness that orchestrates specialized research, strategy, creative, QA, publishing, and analytics agents, with a web dashboard and Photon-powered iMessage/WhatsApp interface.

## 4. Use cases

- B2C growth: Detect TikTok/Instagram trends and create QA-tested product reels.
- B2B demand generation: Find emerging industry conversations and generate educational or product-led content.
- Trend intelligence: Track topics, formats, hooks, audio, competitors, and campaign patterns across social platforms.
- Creative experimentation: Generate multiple video variants and compare their predicted performance before publishing.
- Autonomous content operations: Schedule and publish approved assets, then monitor engagement.
- Self-healing campaigns: Feed QA feedback and real engagement results into future trend analysis, templates, and creative decisions.

## 5. Core system components

- Agent orchestration: Paperclip-inspired organizational hierarchy, task ownership, dependencies, budgets, and approval workflows.
- Trend intelligence: TinyFish-powered discovery and analysis of publicly accessible web and social signals.
- Analytics and visualization: Dashboards showing trends, frequency, engagement signals, competitor patterns, and emerging formats.
- Template intelligence: Agents infer reusable hooks, narratives, structures, editing patterns, and calls to action from observed content.
- Video generation: Video, voiceover, captions, editing, and rendering agents create platform-specific assets.
- QA harness: Persona simulations, factual checks, brand compliance, creative-quality scoring, and revision loops.
- Publishing layer: Platform integrations, scheduling, post status, and human approval controls.
- Feedback loop: Combine QA outcomes, published-post engagement, and fresh external trend data to update future decisions.
- Shared memory: Store trend evidence, template versions, generated assets, QA findings, campaign results, and learnings.

## 6. Agent fleet

- Orchestrator: Plans and coordinates the campaign lifecycle.
- Trend Researcher: Finds and collects trend signals using TinyFish.
- Trend Analyst: Produces insights and data visualizations.
- Pattern Miner: Converts successful content into reusable templates.
- Creative Strategist: Selects concepts, audiences, hooks, and messaging.
- Video Creator: Generates and assembles video assets.
- Persona QA Agents: Simulate distinct audience segments and evaluate creative assets.
- Compliance Reviewer: Flags unsupported claims, brand violations, and platform risks.
- Publisher: Schedules and publishes approved posts.
- Growth Analyst: Measures actual performance and proposes experiments.
- Self-Improvement Agent: Updates templates and strategy based on accumulated evidence.

## 7. Essential workflows

- Trend-to-content: Discover → analyze → visualize → extract patterns → generate content.
- QA and revision: Generate → evaluate → revise → reevaluate → approve.
- Publish and learn: Approve → publish → measure → compare → update strategy.
- Failure recovery: Detect errors → retry or switch approach → log outcome → request human intervention when needed.

## 8. Interfaces

- Web app: Campaign dashboard, trend visualizations, agent activity, video previews, QA reports, approvals, and performance analytics.
- iMessage/WhatsApp agent via Photon: Natural-language campaign creation, progress updates, review requests, approval commands, and performance summaries.

## 9. Success metrics

- Time from trend discovery to approved content.
- Percentage of generated content passing QA.
- Revision count and recurring QA failure types.
- Human approval and rejection rates.
- Publishing success rate and workflow failure recovery.
- Engagement rate, watch time, clicks, conversions, and other platform-specific outcomes.
- Improvement in actual campaign performance across iterations.

## 10. Guardrails

Require explicit authorization before publishing, respect platform APIs and access restrictions, avoid deceptive engagement or impersonation, protect credentials, and distinguish simulated persona feedback from real audience validation.
